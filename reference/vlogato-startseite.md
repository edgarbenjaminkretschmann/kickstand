## I learned following things:

* Never drive between 11 AM and 2 PM unless you really have to when the sun is shining
* Eat street food to save money, but be prepared- it’s mostly the same
* Use all platforms to save money and live comfortably: Google, Agoda, AirBnB
* Take a Motorcycle for the cheapest and most fun way!
