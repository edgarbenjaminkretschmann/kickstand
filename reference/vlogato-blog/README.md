## Story facts for an "about Edgar and his trips" page

- Who: Edgar, "traveling the world on-off for the past 5 years" (About Me, snapshot 2022). Studied Politics and Economics, then did a one-month seminar in Prague to teach English.
- Money: he financed travel by teaching languages, German (always in demand) and English. First job in Qingdao, China (almost a year), then South Korea (also started teaching German), a year of jobs in Germany, then the Philippines, where schools needed German teachers because of the wave of nurses relocating to Germany.
- First bigger journey: Australia, where he saved enough to travel South East Asia. "Going back is never an option." The journey "transformed me". Countries on his site: Philippines, Myanmar, Vietnam.
- Why the Philippines: his "winter refuge": easy visa, English widely spoken, and "the people are very, very friendly".
- Main trip on the site: Philippines, October 2019, by motorcycle with his girlfriend. The homepage says 21 places in 35 days. Route in the posts: Iloilo (Panay) - Dumangas (14 Oct, ferry to Bacolod, about 2 h, about 350 pesos with bike) - Bacolod (15 Oct, "the Ruins") - Sipalay (about 220 km, safe pace about 50 km/h with many pauses; Sugar Beach on 17 Oct, HuyopHuyop waterfall ride on 18 Oct) - Dumaguete - Siquijor (ferry 1500 pesos for rider plus bike, 550 for a passenger) - Bohol (24 Oct, ferry 350 pesos per person plus about 860 for the bike; Tagbilaran, Chocolate Hills, crossing to Ubay, Dumaluyan beach, Panglao on 26 Oct). Later posts: Leyte (southern Leyte via Maasin), Surigao and Siargao (2020), Olongapo and Subic Bay, Iloilo; these have only videos or no recovered text.
- Bikes: Honda VFR 750 from 1995 (bought for 1300 Euro, ridden in Europe in summer; best trip: the Alps in July 2018 through Bavaria, Austria, Switzerland and France, passes at 2500 m). Yamaha SZ 150 (bought used in Iloilo, 18 months, Panay, Mindoro, Manila and Quezon, "didn't break down once"). Rusi 200 cc (bought Oct 2019 under time pressure, a dirt-style bike; a wrong carburetor caused breakdowns, replaced by a 150 cc one in Siquijor after about 1000 km). A Honda Win rented in Saigon for a 22-day Vietnam trip, "broke down only once a day", mechanics on every corner for under 10 USD.
- Memorable moments: running out of fuel and being driven to the pump by a stranger; fixing a dirty spark plug on the road with a toolbox; a farmer-mechanic cleaning the plug in a remote village; a snapped throttle cable near the Chocolate Hills fixed in a workshop that had a real monkey; sleeping under a mosquito net near Dumaguete with an ox and dogs outside; a rainbow at Dumaluyan beach.
- Costs he liked noting: a spark plug change for 55 pesos (about a dollar); Siquijor hostel at about 5 dollars a night with 1 dollar pancakes and shakes; a Bohol hotel with a pool for 1000 pesos (about 15 USD).
- Lessons (homepage and posts): never ride between 11 AM and 2 PM in the sun; eat street food to save money; use Google, Agoda and Airbnb; a motorcycle is the cheapest and most fun way. Cover all skin, the sun was too much. Start earlier. From the Rusi: "get smart about essential things", if you have a bad feeling gather more information, and "don't buy things under pressure, there will always be another option out there".
- Buying habit: he buys almost everything used (instruments, electronics, vehicles, clothes), finding it cheap and environmentally friendly; Facebook Marketplace is his place to find used bikes.
- Gear: Fujifilm X100F and older X100, Sony RX100 VII, Joby GorillaPod, Rode VideoMicro, Zoom H1, a Samsung S9 Plus or S10 phone for navigation and vlogging, and SPF 50 sunscreen on a carabiner on the handlebar.
