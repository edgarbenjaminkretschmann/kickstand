Our Ride to Dumaguete..

Drive to Dumaguete

We had 2 Problems with the motorbike along the way. I underestimated the fuel consumption on the bike and so we got stranded one time without fuel. Luckily some guy just drove me to the next fuel station, which was very close by.

In the other incident my spark plug was dirty. That’s caused because I bought a wrong carburator for my bike, which is meant to be installed only in Offroad bikes. With a little help of surrounding people and my on the road toolbox we could fix this issue in a jiffy, and go on.

We arrived at dusk at our booked place near Dumaguete and stayed at an AirBnB with lots of space and an ox, dogs and other animals making wild noises in the night. Sleeping under a moskito net was also a new experience for us.
